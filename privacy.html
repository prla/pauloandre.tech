Privacy

This site collects a small amount of analytics to understand how it's used. Nothing more.

What's collected

Page views, and two custom events: when you switch between light and dark mode, and when you click a "Let's talk" or "Talk to me" link to book a call. There are no forms on this site that collect personal data directly.

How

Analytics run through PostHog, hosted in the EU. Data is stored in your browser's local storage, not cookies, and no personal profile is created unless you separately identify yourself elsewhere.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Netlify, which may retain standard web server logs (like IP address and browser type) briefly as part of normal operation. That's outside PostHog and not something this site controls directly.

Third parties

Booking a call happens on Google Calendar's own booking page. Subscribing to The Hagakure happens on Substack. Both are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Questions

Reach out via LinkedIn or book a call.

This policy describes how the site is actually built today and may be updated as it changes. Last updated August 26, 2026.
