About

I've spent 20+ years building and leading technology organizations, as an engineer, as a manager, and eventually running engineering orgs end to end.

These days I coach and advise product and engineering leaders, from managers stepping into bigger roles to CTOs and founders, working through problems that don't come with a playbook: engineering that's stopped working for reasons nobody can quite name, a leadership role that no longer fits, an organizational decision nobody wants to make. Sometimes I step in as interim or fractional CTO or VP Engineering.

I've worked with technology leaders from Spotify, Hotjar, Enigma, Adverity, Delivery Hero, AMBOSS, Adobe, and Todoist, among others: around 900 coaching sessions with 85 leaders across 30 companies, including several years as resident leadership coach at Hotjar.

I'm based in Berlin. I also write The Hagakure, essays about technology, leadership, and organizations.
