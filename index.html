04 Patterns I see

You’ve probably blamed the team, the tools, or the process. The problem is upstream of all three.

"We adopted AI. PRs are up. The business doesn't feel it."

What's happeningThe bottleneck moved — from writing code to everything around it. Review, decisions, QA, releases. The old process can't absorb the new throughput, and nobody can explain why velocity didn't change.
What I look forWhere AI is actually being used, what it's replacing, what new bottlenecks it's creating downstream, and whether anyone is measuring the second-order effects.
Typical moveStop measuring AI by activity. Connect adoption to a specific business outcome: fewer escalations, faster cycle time, less rework. Then adjust the system around it.

"The team starts too much and finishes too little."

What's happeningTen things in flight, nothing shipping. Every sprint has more carry-over than completion. The team isn't slow. They're spread across too many bets to finish any of them.
What I look forWIP, handoffs, review queues, unclear ownership, decision latency, and whether "priority" actually means anything.
Typical moveReduce parallel work, clarify decision rights, expose blocked work, and create a weekly operating rhythm around finished outcomes.

"Everything feels urgent, but nothing really moves."

What's happeningEverything is urgent, so nothing gets focused effort. Stakeholders add scope mid-cycle because nobody has the authority, or the backing, to say no. The roadmap is a wishlist, not a plan.
What I look forHow often priorities change, how decisions get reopened, who has the authority to say no, and whether there's a forum where tradeoffs are made explicit.
Typical moveMake tradeoffs explicit, install a small set of priority rules, and create one place where the real list lives.

"We hired strong engineers and still don't ship enough."

What's happeningYou hired strong engineers. They're stuck in a system that makes them slow: too many handoffs, too much waiting, too little ownership. The constraint isn't talent. It's the operating model around it.
What I look forCycle time from decision to shipped outcome, how product specs get produced, how cross-team dependencies are coordinated, and where work waits.
Typical moveSurface the real bottleneck, redesign the handoffs around it, and stop adding pressure to a constraint that isn't the problem.
